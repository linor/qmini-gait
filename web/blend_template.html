Weights come from the paper's three-step interpolation (Eqs 8–10): nearest forward/reverse pair on ẋ, nearest turn/strafe pair by polar angle in the normalised (ẏn, θ̇n) plane, then the two intermediates combined by αturn-strafe.

Each contributing clip is sampled at its own registered time ti = Ωi(φ), so contact events always line up. The root is solved from the planted foot rather than integrated from the command, so achieved is what the blended pose actually produces.

Caveat worth watching: this blends poses, whereas the paper blends the gait parameters and re-runs the planner. Pose blending does not preserve the contact constraint, which shows up as the swing-clearance number going negative on strongly mixed commands. Velocity feedback (Sec. 7.1) closes most of the tracking gap; forward+turn together saturate because η deliberately attenuates the turn sample at speed.
